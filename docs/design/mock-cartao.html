What's real and what's not

  • Real: every fountain name, address, kind (bebedouro or chafariz), accessibility and position comes from data/pontos.geojson, around Praça das Flores.
  • Example: walking minutes assume you are standing about 130 m south of the square. Status lines ("confirmado há 2 dias por 3 pessoas") are made up to show the format.
  • Example: the photos are drawn stand-ins. The brief's test case asks for 3 photos; the seed album has 1 for each Flores bebedouro.
  • Hypothesis: the dog bowl and bottle tap chips assume "acessível = nova rede", which is still to be checked on the street.

What to look at

  • Sem info keeps the same photo slot and turns it into the invite, so 90% of cards still look complete.
  • Tem água agora? is the one-tap report. Não opens the choices in place, with no new screen.
  • Map: cluster counters and photo pins stay as they are; walking rings are drawn at 5 and 10 minutes.
  • Desktop: the phone's swipe row becomes the list on the left, and the same card docks in the middle.
